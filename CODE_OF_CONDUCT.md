# Code of Conduct

Schema Commons is built in public, for everyone. We follow a simple version of the Contributor Covenant.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or technology choices.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, assume good faith, and welcome newcomers.
- Critique ideas and schemas, not people.
- Prefer evidence ("here is data that breaks this field") over opinion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the Steering Council (general@observalytics.com). Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban.
